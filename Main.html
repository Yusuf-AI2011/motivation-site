English Speech • Topic 3

Consistency
Over Intensity

Why daily small efforts outperform occasional intense bursts every single time.

Key Speech Hook

"Studying 10 hours in one day won't make you fluent. But studying 20 minutes every single day will build a habit that changes everything."

Slide 1 of 2
Actionable Advice

How to Stay Consistent

01

Never Miss Twice

Missing one day is an accident. Missing two days in a row is the start of a new bad habit.

02

Start Small

Set a minimum daily target so simple that it feels impossible to fail—even on bad days.

03

Trust the Compound

Progress isn't visible on day 1 or day 7. Trust the cumulative effect over months.
